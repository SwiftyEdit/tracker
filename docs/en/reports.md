# Reports

## Choosing a time range

The time range at the top of the overview applies to everything on the page (totals, chart,
tables, breakdowns):

- **7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days**: the last days up to and including today
- **All**: from the first day with data until today
- **from … to …**: a free range, confirmed with **Apply**. The end date can't be in the future.
  An invalid range (e.g. start after end) falls back to the 30-day view.

The range actually shown is printed below the controls.

## Sections

- **Totals**: pageviews, visitors and days with data in the selected range
- **Trend**: chart of pageviews and visitors per day
- **Daily figures**: the same numbers as a table (newest first, scrollable)
- **Top pages**: up to 30 pages by pageviews
- **Breakdowns**: sources (campaigns), referrers, browsers, operating systems, device types and
  countries, the top 20 entries each

## Reading the numbers

- A **visitor** is a value recalculated every day (see *Privacy & technology*). The same person
  on two days counts as two visitors. For multi-day ranges the visitor count is therefore the sum
  of the daily values, not a count of unique people.
- **Referrers** counts every pageview with an external referrer. Internal clicks between pages of
  your own site are not counted (see the *Own domain* setting).
- **Sources** counts only a visitor's first pageview per day (see *Sources & campaigns*).
- Today's numbers can still change until the day is over.

## Refreshing

The statistics are recomputed when you open the overview, at most every five minutes. Fresh
pageviews therefore show up the next time you open it after the last update is more than five
minutes old.
